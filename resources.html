Common Questions

Are these resources really free? What's the catch?

Yes, they're completely free! We provide these resources because they're genuinely useful for businesses exploring automation. We ask for your email so we can follow up with relevant information and offers. If you ever want to unsubscribe, you can do so anytime.

Can we use these templates for our internal team planning?

Absolutely! The templates are designed to be used in internal workshops, planning sessions, and team exercises. They help align your team on process mapping and automation opportunity identification. Feel free to customize them for your use case.

Are the ROI calculator numbers realistic for my business?

The calculator provides estimates based on industry averages and case studies from 50+ SME implementations. Actual results depend on your specific context: process complexity, team adoption, and implementation timeline. For a personalized assessment, book a discovery call with our team.

What's the difference between templates and professional guidance?

Templates give you the structure and methodology. Professional guidance adds the domain expertise: knowing which automations deliver the fastest ROI, avoiding common mistakes, handling edge cases, and managing implementation risks. Think of templates as the map—professional services help you navigate it efficiently.

Can I share these resources with my leadership team?

Of course! That's actually the best use case for these resources. Share the ROI calculator with your CFO, the readiness guide with your CTO, and the templates with your ops team. Each person will get value tailored to their perspective.
