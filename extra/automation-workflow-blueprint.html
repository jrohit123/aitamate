About This Blueprint

This template helps you visualize what an automated process looks like after optimization. It documents:

  • Triggers: What starts the workflow?
  • Actions: What does the automation do?
  • Decisions: Where does logic branch (if X, then Y)?
  • Data flows: What information moves between systems?
  • Notifications: Who gets alerted and when?
Best approach: Use this right after your current-state process audit. Map what SHOULD happen, not what currently happens. This becomes your design spec.
