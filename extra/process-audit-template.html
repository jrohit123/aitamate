How to Use This Template

This template enables you to document a single business process from end-to-end. Complete one template per process you want to audit or automate.

Typical use case: You've identified a high-priority process (e.g., customer onboarding, invoice processing, hiring workflow). Now map out every step, every tool, every decision, and every handoff.

Key participants: Include someone who actually does the work (not just a manager). They'll catch the steps that technically don't exist but happen anyway.

Time required: 60-90 minutes to document a process. More time for complex enterprise workflows.

Automation Readiness Assessment

Rate this process on readiness for automation (1-5). 1 = Too complex or unstable, 5 = Ideal candidate.

5 = Never changes, 1 = Changes every week

5 = Perfect (no cleanup needed), 1 = Messy and inconsistent

5 = All systems have APIs, 1 = Lots of legacy systems, no APIs

5 = Crystal clear, 1 = Complex and ad-hoc

5 = Major savings or revenue impact, 1 = Minimal benefit

This template is deliberately incomplete. You now have the structure. An automation consultant would:

  • Deep-dive on the 2-3 most painful steps
  • Get technical details about API availability and data formats
  • Run scenarios: "If we automate steps 3-5, what does the new process look like?"
  • Provide specifics on tools, cost, timeline, and expected ROI
