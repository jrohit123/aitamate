This Privacy Policy explains how aitamate Solutions collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our website (aitamate.com), our products (ProValuate and Momentum), or engage with our services. Please read it carefully. By using our platforms, you agree to the practices described here.

Section 01

Who We Are

aitamate Solutions ("aitamate", "we", "us", or "our") is a business transformation company providing AI-powered products and services. Our registered business operates internationally, with primary operations governed by applicable Indian law under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its associated rules, including the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules").

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, we also comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR respectively. For users in other jurisdictions, we apply the higher of local law requirements or our default standards described in this policy.

For the purposes of data protection law, aitamate Solutions is the data controller for personal data collected via our website and services.

Section 02

Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following categories, depending on how you interact with us:

Category Examples Source
Identity Data First name, last name, job title, company name Contact form, product sign-up
Contact Data Work email address Contact form, newsletter sign-up, product sign-up
Enquiry Data Message content, area of interest, team size Contact form
Product Usage Data Feature interactions, check-in submissions, hiring assessments, performance data, login timestamps ProValuate and Momentum platforms
Billing & Payment Data Subscription plan, billing address, transaction reference Payment gateway (we do not store full card details)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (such as health information, caste, religion, or biometric data) through our website or products. If any such data is submitted through free-text fields, we will delete it upon discovery.

Section 03

How We Use Your Data

We use the personal data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries submitted via our contact form and to schedule discovery calls or demos.
  • To deliver our products and services — including operating ProValuate and Momentum accounts, processing assessments, and providing platform functionality.
  • To send marketing communications — including newsletters, product updates, and educational content — where you have given explicit consent.
  • To process payments for subscriptions or service engagements via our authorised payment gateway partner(s).
  • To improve our products — using aggregated, anonymised usage data to understand how our platforms are used and where improvements can be made.
  • To comply with legal obligations — including maintaining records required under applicable law and responding to lawful requests from authorities.
  • To protect the security of our platforms and detect fraudulent or abusive activity.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties, and we will not use it for purposes incompatible with those stated above without first obtaining your consent.

Section 04

Legal Basis for Processing

For users in the EEA or UK, our processing of personal data relies on the following legal bases under GDPR:

  • Consent — for marketing emails and newsletter sign-ups. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract — to fulfil our obligations when you sign up for ProValuate, Momentum, or a service engagement.
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to enquiries, prevent fraud, improve our products, and operate our business, where such interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required to comply with applicable laws.

For users in India, our processing is governed by the IT Act 2000 and SPDI Rules, which require that sensitive personal data is collected with informed consent and used only for the purpose for which it was collected.

Section 05

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — trusted third-party vendors who process data on our behalf (e.g., cloud hosting, email delivery, customer support tools), bound by data processing agreements that restrict their use of your data.
  • Payment processors — your payment data is handled directly by our authorised gateway partner(s). See Section 6 for details.
  • Legal requirements — if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, we may disclose data where legally compelled.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify affected users in advance.

All third-party data processors are contractually required to maintain appropriate security standards and to process data only as instructed by us.

Section 06

Payment Processing

All payment transactions on aitamate's platforms are processed by authorised third-party payment gateway providers (which may include Razorpay, Stripe, or equivalent PCI-DSS compliant processors).

We do not store, process, or transmit full card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account credentials on our own servers. Your financial information is handled directly and securely by the payment gateway under their own privacy and security policies.

We retain only the minimum billing metadata required for accounting and compliance purposes — such as your subscription plan, billing address, transaction reference number, and payment status.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of the relevant payment processor at the time of your transaction.

Section 07

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses a limited set of cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate correctly and understand how visitors use our site.

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function (e.g., session management, form security). These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional cookies — remember your preferences and settings to improve your experience.
  • Analytics cookies — collect anonymised, aggregated data about how pages are used. We use this to improve site performance and content.

We do not currently use advertising or retargeting cookies (e.g., Meta Pixel or Google Ads). If this changes, this policy will be updated and appropriate consent mechanisms will be implemented.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our website.

Section 08

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Our general retention guidelines are:

  • Contact form enquiries — retained for 2 years from the date of last meaningful interaction, after which they are securely deleted.
  • Marketing opt-in records — retained for the duration of your subscription to our communications, plus a further 2 years for audit purposes.
  • Product usage data — retained for the duration of your active account, plus 90 days following account closure to allow for data export requests.
  • Billing records — retained for 7 years to comply with financial record-keeping obligations under applicable law.
  • Technical/server logs — retained for up to 90 days for security monitoring purposes.

When retention periods expire, data is either securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Section 09

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (GDPR users).
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw marketing consent at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at sales@aitamate.com. We will respond within 30 days (or within the timeframe required by applicable law). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

If you are located in the EEA and believe we have not adequately addressed your concerns, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Section 10

International Data Transfers

aitamate operates internationally, and your personal data may be processed or stored in countries outside your own — including countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Where we transfer personal data outside the EEA or UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission;
  • Adequacy decisions by the relevant data protection authority; or
  • Other legally recognised transfer mechanisms.

For users in India, we comply with the cross-border data transfer provisions of the IT Act and applicable rules, and we take reasonable steps to ensure equivalent data protection standards are maintained by our international partners.

Section 11

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction, or disclosure. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest;
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel only;
  • Regular review of our information security practices;
  • Use of reputable, security-certified cloud infrastructure providers.

While we take all reasonable precautions, no system is completely immune to security risks. In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law — within 72 hours where GDPR applies.

Section 12

Children's Privacy

Our website and products are intended for use by business professionals and are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a person under 18, please contact us immediately at sales@aitamate.com and we will delete it promptly.

Section 13

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page;
  • Notify active product users via email or in-platform notification where the changes are significant.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of our website or products after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
