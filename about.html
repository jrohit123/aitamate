About aitamate

Built From Experience. Driven by Purpose.

aitamate was born from a simple but powerful conviction: that AI should be accessible, ethical, and genuinely useful — for businesses of every size.

Our Vision

To unleash human creativity with the power of Augmented Intelligence.

We believe AI should expand what humans are capable of — not replace what makes us irreplaceable. Our vision is a world where every business, regardless of size or resources, can access intelligent tools that free people to do their best work.

Our Mission

To catalyse AI adoption and make intelligent process optimisation accessible to businesses of all sizes.

We're not here to sell technology for its own sake. We're here to help real businesses solve real problems — with AI solutions that are practical, ethical, and built to last.

The Founder

24+ Years in HR & Operations. One Unavoidable Conclusion.

After spending over 24 years working across HR leadership and operations strategy, Rohit Jain kept encountering the same problem in every organisation he worked with: talented people were spending their most valuable hours on work that should never have been on their plate in the first place.

The tools existed to fix this. The data was there. But no one had built a solution that was accessible, practical, and built on genuine domain expertise rather than technology for its own sake. So Rohit built it himself.

aitamate is the result — a company built at the intersection of deep HR and operations know-how and purposeful AI engineering. Every product and service aitamate offers was designed to solve problems Rohit has personally experienced and witnessed costing organisations millions in lost potential.

"I didn't build aitamate to be a technology company. I built it to be a business transformation company that uses technology intelligently."
